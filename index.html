05 — Codes & Standards

Where the numbers come from.

Every calculator, the method it uses and the code, standard or published source behind it — so you know what to check before a result goes into a design.

How these references were compiled. The formulas and values in MechToolBox were written from established engineering knowledge of the standards listed here — they were not transcribed page by page from licensed copies. Editions differ, local amendments apply, and some values are typical rather than code-mandated. Always confirm against the edition your jurisdiction has adopted.

      Local codes govern.
